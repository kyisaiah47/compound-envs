Convex pricing

Reactive TypeScript backend — database, functions and realtime in one.

  • Starter 1M included
  • Starter 0.5 GB included
  • Professional $25 per developer/month
  • Professional $2 per additional 1M
  • Professional $0.20 per additional GB

Pricing is billed monthly in arrears and every figure on this page is what the account is charged in United States dollars. Usage above an included allowance is metered and appears on the following statement. Annual agreements are available for teams that want a fixed commitment, and support response times are stated in the service agreement rather than here. Taxes are added where the account's billing address requires them. Nothing on this page is a quote and the figures are reviewed whenever a plan changes.
